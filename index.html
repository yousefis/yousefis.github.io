Sahar Yousefi

Senior Computer Vision and AI Scientist at Philips

I’m a Senior Computer Vision and AI Scientist at Philips, where I help lead AI development for DeviceGuide, an image-guided therapy solution that supports clinicians during heart valve procedures. I hold a PhD in Artificial Intelligence. Previously, I was Lead Computer Vision Engineer at Prime Vision, working on document understanding with large language models. At AutoFill Technologies, I was Head of AI Development, working on machine-vision technology to automate inspections in the automotive industry. Earlier, I was a postdoctoral researcher at Leiden University Medical Center.

Selected projects

  • Donut-hole: A distilled, sparsified version of the Donut document-understanding model that reduces model density by 54% while preserving performance.
  • fastMRI: A NeurIPS 2019 research project organized by Facebook AI Research to investigate using AI to make MRI scans up to 10× faster.
  • DDAUnet: Dilated Dense Attention U-Net for esophageal GTV segmentation.
  • ASL-to-PET Translation: A CNN-based model for translating ASL to PET images.
  • Hadamard-te-ASL Reconstruction: Dynamic angiography and perfusion reconstruction from rank-8 Hadamard-te arterial spin labeling.
  • Motion Detector: A motion detection method using the 3D discrete wavelet transform.
